Welcome to my blog! I've been meaning to start writing for a while now, and I finally decided to stop procrastinating and just do it.

## Why Start a Blog?

There are a few reasons I wanted to create this space:

1. **Learning by teaching** - Writing forces you to truly understand a topic. If you can't explain something clearly, you probably don't understand it as well as you think.

2. **Documentation for future me** - How many times have you solved a problem, only to encounter the same issue months later and forget the solution? This blog is partly a searchable archive for myself.

3. **Sharing knowledge** - If something I write helps even one person solve a problem or learn something new, that's a win.

## What to Expect

I'll be writing about software development, with a focus on:

- Data and backend engineering
- Developer tools and productivity
- Programming best practices
- Probably some AI stuff
- Occasionally, thoughts on the broader tech industry

I'm aiming for quality over quantity. I'd rather publish one well-researched, genuinely useful article per month than pump out shallow content every week.
